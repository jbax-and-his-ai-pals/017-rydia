# Where to find each monster

Generated by `python tools/export_encounters.py` from the clean ROM; don't edit by hand. Per-slot data is in [../data/encounters.csv](../data/encounters.csv).

- Each place lists the chance that a random fight there includes the monster.
  - Locations show their best floor. World-map areas that share an encounter list are merged.
  - How often fights happen differs by place too; the `rate` column in the CSV has it.
- **†** marks places that may be out of reach in this hack:
  - no chain of doors links them to a world-map entrance, so a scene or warp takes you there (Tower of Zot, Lunar Subterrane, most of the underworld Tower of Bab-il)
  - or they're story-only: the Giant of Babil, whose arc counts as done in the late-game state.
- "unknown map" rooms have no title and no doors to a named map. "No triggers" rooms have no doors, exits or events at all and are likely unused.
- Only `capturable=y` species from `data/monsters.csv` are listed.
