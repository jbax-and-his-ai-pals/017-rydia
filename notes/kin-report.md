## Capture data

- `capture_tier`: 1 (easy) to 5 (hard), seeded from level (≤9, ≤19, ≤34, ≤59, 60+); boss-bit species are 5.
- `capture_likes`: conditions that raise this species' odds. `capture_needs`: hard requirements, for a few rare species only. Both are semicolon lists, empty until curated.
- Vocabulary: `sleep`, `charm`, `confuse`, `paralyze`, `slow`, `stop`, `blind`, `poison`, `mute`, `low_hp`, `critical_hp`, `alone`, `level_gap`. `alone` means the last monster standing; `level_gap` means Rydia's level is well above the monster's.
